**Crucial prior scholarship not to misclaim as original:** [Cheryl I. Harris (1993), *Whiteness as Property*](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/harris1993.md), established the juridical-property account of whiteness; [Heidi Nicholls (2026)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/nicholls2026.md) demonstrates both segregating and amalgamating eugenic settler sociology, while [Musab Younis (2026)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/younis2026.md) traces demography's colonial political genealogy. The manuscript's proposed contribution is the different question of revealed preference when *actual subjects' generational continuity*, *imagined future reproductive legitimacy*, and *elite/property continuity* conflict. The evidence so far demonstrates the contradiction's intelligibility, not that a whole white-classified population was historically sacrificed unto extinction.

**Virginia legislative boundary:** The [1924 enacted law](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/virginia-assembly1924.md) contained the one-sixteenth Indigenous ancestry exception; a [1926 House proposal](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/virginia-humanities1926.md) sought and obtained an amendment expanding it to one-eighth. The **Senate indefinitely postponed that proposal**. It was a meaningful contested *legislative preference*, not an enacted replacement for the 1924 law. The history must also attend to the [dispossession of Indigenous identity and community recognition](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/nps-racial-integrity.md) and [Arica L. Coleman's Black-Indigenous histories](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/coleman2013.md); white elite family status was not the sole harmed or protected interest.

**Materiality and demographic counterevidence:** [Haines and Hacker (2006)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/haines-hacker2006.md) report a declining white child–woman ratio in 1800–1860, but [Hacker (2003)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/hacker2003.md) dates the onset of **reconstructed fertility** decline differently. [Hacker, Haines and Jaremski (2021)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/hacker-haines-jaremski2021.md) compare explanations using complete-count censuses; these are competing estimator designs, not proof that purifying ideology caused demographic shortfall. The [direct coerced reproduction study by Djeliid (2024)](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/djeliid2024.md) and [Virginia's 1662 statute](https://github.com/lenticule-science/science-services-section/blob/737a615ce674f19e58f56ec6070375c8d027b2e0/services/social-science-services-section/services/sociology-science-services-section/content/text/documents/articles/bibliographies/virginia-assembly1662.md) concern different but materially connected mechanisms by which actual existing lives reproduced slaveholding property, kinship harm and labor.
