# White increase and the self-consuming reproductive ideal

## The opening proposition: an ideal that can outlast its reproductive substrate

> One thing that’s a bit deeper than I have time for, is studying the reality of white increase in settler societies with the theory being that even if we assume whiteness to be a real thing, what’s real about it is that it’s willing to grind down the white population to oblivion if it means finally yielding at least two viable super specimens from which potential breeding can be generated which obvious will never happen before “white” ppl “demographically” pass away first lol

**Leader conjecture (untested, conditional):** Even if the category *white* is granted reality for the argument, the institutional system treating it as real might be willing to exhaust the reproduction of actually existing white-classified people in pursuit of an indefinitely deferred ideal. This is *not* the claim that whiteness constitutes a discrete human genetic species. What may be real is the category's power over lives, property, marriage, family, inheritance, movement, bodies and classifications. "Willing" is an institutional revealed preference, not a literal consciousness: show policy decisions that preserve an imaginary ideal or class property even when they reduce the reproductive survival of the people invoked to justify those decisions.

**The phrase "at least two viable super specimens" is a minimum promised end-condition, not a proposal to breed precisely two people.** Earlier emphasis on their eventual inbreeding coefficient answered a different question. The central question is whether a sequence of exclusions can ever produce the supposedly legitimate future before the actual population supporting the search ceases to reproduce itself. This makes **time to achieve the ideal** and **time to lose reproductive continuity** independent clocks.

Let W(t) be people classified white under a specific dated social rule; A(t) the members whom an institution's rule I(t) calls fit to reproduce; and R(t) the real opportunities for supported reproduction, including surviving cohorts, kinship recognition, care, wages, housing, and migration. These are not species or natural breeding categories. Define τ_ideal as the earliest t at which a historically documented I(t) is satisfied by a group of **at least two** actually viable prospective progenitors, according to the regime's own supposed success test. Define τ_continuity as the earliest t at which the chosen population or cohort fails a separately specified continuity condition (which must say whether this means fertility below replacement, decline of locally born descendants, reclassification, or literal absence). The core **timing hypothesis** is that in some regimes τ_continuity precedes τ_ideal; an additional hypothesis is that I(t) itself keeps changing so τ_ideal never arrives. No race's numerical extinction follows without evidence and clear boundaries.

A **moving-target process** would occur if the unsuccessful search repeatedly tightens I(t+1), shrinking A(t) and consuming supported reproduction rather than revising the idea of perfection. Success in a historical population is not measured by two imagined genetic outliers. Evidence for this process requires the *causal explanation for each tightening*, not simply a list of sterilizations. Incorporation, changing category membership, positive-natalist supports and successful cohort replacement are essential countercases. A regime can also pursue criteria for elite inheritance or status rather than any achievable reproductive end condition. Then endless deferral may be materially useful to the governing class even if its official racial objective is unattainable.

### The remainder of the original paragraph supplies the actual reproductive substrate

Local natural increase, net migration of prospective parents, births into hereditary captivity and changes in racial classification must be measured separately. Immigration may import the parents of a later locally born generation. Births to captive mothers could reproduce slaveholder property without reproducing the child's free political membership; coercion, family formation, and legal status must be distinguished rather than equating every enslaved birth with a specialized breeding plantation. Indigenous families and sovereignty are not reducible to settler statistical coverage. These processes could reproduce *settler society* even if a particular population of settlers was not reproducing itself without imports. The historical U.S. as a whole did experience substantial natural increase; the proposition must be period- and category-specific rather than a universal assertion of immigration dependence.

### A historical triangle confronting the hypothesis

**Franklin, 1751.** In *Observations Concerning the Increase of Mankind*, §§1–3, Franklin states that in some cities deaths outnumber births and local natural generation does not supply the inhabitants. §§23–24 connect settler migration to a wish to increase the number of people he called purely white, while excluding from that term many continental Europeans, Africans and Indigenous people. This shows distinct **birth**, **migration**, **territorial expansion** and **racial-membership selection** processes within early settler political arithmetic. The written wish to increase a narrow category is not evidence of its later decline. [Source](../../../../bibliographies/franklin1751.md).

**Nearing, 1911.** In *"Race Suicide" vs. Overpopulation*, Nearing challenges race-suicide rhetoric by openly counterposing fewer births and supposedly better population quality. This is a contemporary statement of the **quantity versus claimed quality** conflict, not genetic proof that such categories are objective or that an extinction feedback was actually operating. [Source](../../../../bibliographies/nearing1911.md).

**Roosevelt, 1913.** His letter to Charles Davenport endorses encouraging reproduction by the "right type" and preventing it among the "wrong type." It confirms the *coexistence* of competing reproductive directives—expand one group and exclude others—including people a racial elite itself judged unfit. It does not quantify their effects. [Source](../../../../bibliographies/roosevelt1913.md).

**Younis, 2026.** *Lineages of a conspiracy: the "great replacement" and demography* locates modern population science within the colonial management of territory, labor and racialized demographic fears, tracing Petty and Franklin. It adds the crucial warning that white demographic catastrophe can be a politically mobilized fiction that reinforces hierarchy, not a measured demographic fact. This essay does not demonstrate the Leader's timing hypothesis. [Source](../../../../bibliographies/younis2026.md).

### The measurements actually needed

1. **Preference sacrifice:** a policy choice that demonstrably maintains a selective reproductive ideal or settler class order while reducing the reproduction of nominal beneficiaries, despite a feasible less-destructive alternative.
2. **Moving goal:** the time series of eligibility rules, their rationales and observed changes; proof that prior nonattainment *caused* further tightening, distinguished from periodic reclassification or expansion.
3. **Remaining runway:** cohort-specific births, deaths, future age structure, family formation and material support, plus foreign-born and locally born prospective parents. The mere fraction of operations targeting whites is not a fertility-denominator estimate.
4. **Other people's reproduction:** the contribution of captive births and immigration to the actual settler society, dated and classified in ways that do not erase Indigenous populations outside early censuses.
5. **Counterexamples:** expanding legal whiteness, pro-natalist success, stable criteria, and growing white-category population despite coercive programs. The North Carolina evidence below already defeats a universal historical collapse claim.

The result sought is a comparison of **two empirical timescales and a changing institutional objective**, not another isolated percentage of people sterilized, nor a literal genetic model of two founders.

## Statement to explain, not flatten

**Leader's hypothesis (unassayed).** Even granting the premise that whiteness could be real, its institutional selection rules might consume the actual population it claims to preserve while continually promising an eventual founding set of *at least two* ideal reproducers. The timing of reproductive continuity failure relative to a changing, possibly unreachable selection goal—not the eventual pair's pedigree—is the main conjecture. No white genetic essence or inevitable extinction is asserted.

The question comes **before** the interesting but downstream estimate of births into U.S. slavery. Captive births matter here because their reproductive futures could reproduce settler wealth and political power without reproducing membership of the white population. Do not answer the opening question by fact-checking the birth percentage.

## Four independent meanings of reproduction

- **Human population reproduction**: births, deaths, survival, family formation, migration and descendants among actual human beings. No isolated biological race population is presumed.
- **Reproduction of nominal whiteness**: continued membership of a historically contingent category. Legal rules, administrative racial assignment and social incorporation affect this count as well as births and migration.
- **Reproduction of admissible whiteness**: the subset whose sexual, familial and reproductive conduct is deemed capable of producing the *desirable* white future by a racialized regime. This can be narrower than nominal whiteness and can shift within it.
- **Reproduction of white settler power**: continued acquisition and control of land, wages, property claims, inheritances, captive labor, electoral authority and state institutions. This can continue through the lives and reproduction of people denied white status.

**The key nonidentity:** persons classified white are not identical to persons whose reproduction is supported or authorized, and neither set is identical to the beneficiaries or sources of the institution's material reproduction.

## The first mechanism: internal purification

An institution claiming to preserve whites can also maintain an internal border against whites it calls unfit. Poverty, disability, religion, sex, phenotype, national origin and alleged moral defects can be treated as grounds to exclude reproduction or family recognition. This is a *second selection* performed inside a nominally privileged category.

- Anna Stubblefield (2007) shows how U.S. eugenic classifications of alleged feeblemindedness combined poverty, disabilities, off-white ethnicities and gendered moral judgments as **tainted whiteness**. This is direct evidence of internal white exclusion, not evidence of an extinction trajectory.
- Simone M. Caron (2008) reconstructs the *race suicide* ideology, which encouraged fecundity among the supposedly fit white Protestant elite while seeking to reduce births among people classified unfit. This is a documented conflict between total fertility and selective eligibility.
- Rickie Solinger (2017) and Melissa Murray (2022) describe reproductive regulation by race and class, including coercive sterilization, and the distinction between restriction of someone else's fertility and autonomous contraceptive/abortion decisions.
- The *race suicide* diagnosis normally **blamed** other populations' fertility and preferred white women's alleged failure to reproduce. The distinct conjecture here is that the very response—selective exclusion—can undercut the reproductive capacity the proponents claim to defend.

To call the process a **reproductive purity ratchet**, more than one exclusionary policy must be observed. A ratchet requires an endogenous feedback loop: each failure of real people to match the imagined ideal produces a further restriction rather than revising the ideal. Historically there are also countervailing tendencies to incorporate immigrants and encourage large white families. They can interrupt or reverse the proposed ratchet. Those counterexamples belong in the theory.

The language of whiteness being "willing" to grind down whites is a precise shorthand only if translated into observable decisions. Ask what politicians, professionals, courts and institutions *actually sacrifice when values conflict*, not what a personified race inwardly wants.

## Why the two-super-specimen limit fails biologically and conceptually

Two unrelated adult founders do not constitute a sustainable long-term genetic population under ordinary human sexual reproduction. Assuming both reproduced, their descendants would soon share ancestors, while small effective population size magnifies drift and inbreeding. Conservation genetics models (Pérez-Pereira and colleagues, 2022, with subsequent correction) concern minimum viable effective population sizes generally far above two, with thresholds sensitive to reproductive rate and assumptions. They are **not racial population genetics**, and one cannot convert their estimates into numbers for a fictitious biologically pure white race.

The more interesting contradiction is conceptual: an imagined **individual optimum** is neither a realistic **population optimum** nor a coherent criterion for social legitimacy. A breeding population needs diverse, surviving, voluntarily reproducing persons and supportive conditions; selecting an imagined exemplary *type* cannot supply those requirements. The regime may preserve its hierarchy by excluding supposedly defective people even when the exclusion makes its own promised future less likely.

## A minimal falsifiable thought model

Let W(t) be the population classified white under an explicitly dated rule C(t), E(t) the subset the regime recognizes as legitimate reproducers, q(t) = |E(t)| / |W(t)| its eligibility share, and r(t) the average number of surviving entrants to W generated per selected adult under the model.

For a **closed, unrealistically simplified example** in which only selected adults reproduce, there is no migration or reclassification, and all descendants enter W:

    W(t+1) = r(t) q(t) W(t).

This is not an empirical U.S. population equation; it makes the selection conflict visible. Even if r(t) is positive, W declines if r(t) q(t) < 1. Tightening eligibility can reduce q(t); to establish the hypothesized causal process, one must also show that this restriction actually reduced surviving descendants and was not offset by pro-natalism, reclassification, migration or fertility outside the ideal subset. Never infer actual national births from this conditional identity.

For observed population change, record births, deaths, migration and reclassification separately, under consistent jurisdictions and categories. For institutional reproduction, use another outcome P(t) such as property/control/apportionment; P(t+1) may grow even if W(t+1) < W(t). A category can also expand by assimilation while E contracts under new eugenic criteria. **No arrow from one outcome to the others is justified without a mechanism.**

## The second mechanism: white ancestry need not reproduce white political membership

The colonial property regime made this particularly stark. In jurisdictions applying *partus sequitur ventrem*, the child of a woman held enslaved inherited her captive legal condition, even when the father was white. The ancestry of white men could therefore contribute to children registered and treated not as white citizens/heirs but as people subject to property claims. Biological reproduction, institutional reproduction and racialized legal inclusion diverged.

Jeffory Clymer (2012) traces contradictions among maternal legal status, asserted "blood" identity and property title. Hortense Spillers (1987; already in this Sociology bibliography) supplies a deeper account of slavery's assault on recognized kinship. Gwendoline Alphonso's 2023 **unreviewed preprint** argues that antebellum courts valued enslaved women's *future increase* as a distinct and alienable property claim. This is a mechanism by which another person's possible descendants can be appropriated in advance.

Consequently "white increase" can mean more white *ancestry* while recording fewer socially white *offspring*, and meanwhile more **slaveholder property**. This conclusion concerns differentiated status rules and inheritance, not a stable genetic definition of whiteness. Do not universalize a modern "one-drop rule" backward across all historical jurisdictions. Do not infer that every enslaved pregnancy was industrially organized by an enslaver, or erase enslaved people's own kinship, resistance or desires.

J. David Hacker (2020) reconstructs births into U.S. slavery by decade; those numbers are central to any captive-birth numerator. Marianne H. Wanamaker (2014) gives an economically specific substitution model: slave labor and white slaveholders' own children were alternative household production inputs, and changing child prices were associated with fertility responses in slaveowning families. This does not show that slavery caused a universal decline of white fertility, but it is an example of economic class reproduction becoming less dependent upon a household's own descendants.

## The third mechanism: territory, classification and settlement

Settler reproduction is not equivalent to white births. Patrick Wolfe (2006) shows why Indigenous political-territorial continuity and its elimination must be distinguished from the labor extraction of hereditary slavery, even when both sustain a settler state. Indigenous sovereignty is neither created by nor reducible to colonial enumeration.

Immigration imports adults and prospective parents; later children born within the colony appear in its *natural increase* even when the reproductive opportunity arose from an imperial migration network. Thomas A. Guglielmo (2003) documents changing racial and color categories and status among Italian immigrants, making clear that administrative whiteness can change without any new white births. Some racial orders seek exclusion and others incorporation/whitening. Their different policies are empirical counterexamples to a single monotonically contracting racial boundary.

Historic U.S. censuses do not count every person on the territory on equal terms, including many Indigenous people excluded as "Indians not taxed." A denominator built from that enumeration is not all births on lands now called the United States.

## Tests, falsifiers, boundaries

**H1: Selection/continuity conflict.** In a named historical eugenic regime, increasing reproductive exclusion within nominal whiteness measurably reduced its descendants despite publicly articulated white-survival objectives. Compare policy chronology, eligibility, family formation, observed births/survival, pro-natalist offsets, class confounders and control jurisdictions. *Falsifier:* there is no credible reduction, or interventions were compensated and the selected population retained continuity.

**H2: Endogenous purity ratchet.** Reproductive exclusions were repeatedly tightened as earlier conditions failed to produce a satisfactory racialized ideal. Code each law, institutional rationale and rule-change date; test for feedback rather than assume it from successive restrictions. *Falsifier:* criteria stabilized, became broader, or changed for unrelated reasons.

**H3: Reproduction without membership.** Institutions converted the reproduction of people outside legally recognized whiteness into settler wealth/power: hereditary slavery/property, electoral apportionment, land relations and differential family recognition. Test through probate, state law, petitions, household records and political apportionment with explicit temporal/jurisdictional boundaries. *Falsifier:* a named conversion mechanism did not apply in that setting.

**H4: Boundary adaptation.** When numerical demographic decline threatens the category, the institution sometimes recruits/integrates new members rather than continuing to contract. Trace naturalization, census classifications, marriage law, class/access and official race meanings. This alternative mechanism may dominate the ratchet.

Use comparative case design rather than silently imposing the United States on Canada, Australia, South Africa or Latin America. Never conflate coercive sterilization of a poor white woman, a captive Black mother's status, and disruption of Indigenous familial sovereignty: they are legally and materially different violences.

## Demography remains a subordinate research task

The earlier conjecture that 25% of all U.S. births in 1790–1850 occurred to enslaved mothers was not substantiated by the cited reconstruction; a preliminary combination of historical estimates suggested something closer to one-sixth. Before adopting *any* such percentage as fact, rebuild the numerator and denominator for matched 1790–1849 versus 1790–1850 calendar windows, shifting U.S. boundaries, whose births were counted, excluded Indigenous populations, estimated mortality, and uncertainty bands. It cannot be generalized as a share of all human births on the territory. And no percentage alone establishes deliberate industrial breeding, though hereditary commodification industrialized the expropriation of reproductive futures.

## Quantitative case study: sterilization inside whiteness (2026-10-17)

**Result and limit.** Archival records document the deliberate exclusion of thousands of people classified *white* from future reproduction. Those exclusions were neither synonymous with the total reproductive dynamics of white-classified populations nor a fixed, monotonically narrowing racial-selection policy. The numeric source ledger lives at [white-increase-eugenic-selection-measurements-2026-10-17.json](../../../../../../../data/records/white-increase-eugenic-selection-measurements-2026-10-17.json); it records exact original integers, operations, formulas, and observation limits. The following figures are descriptive administrative observations, not estimates of white births prevented or biological "fitness."

### Number 1: 4,218 sterilization operations recorded for the white-classified population in North Carolina, July 1929–June 1966

The North Carolina Eugenics Board's own 1964–1966 report, *Table 16*, records **6,851** cumulative sterilization operations, **4,218** on people administratively recorded as white (61.57% of operations), 2,590 on people it classified Black, and 43 other. The 4,218 white-classified operations include **3,436 performed on women** (81.46%) and 782 on men, determined from the listed operations (3,409 salpingectomies + 27 ovariectomies, versus 746 vasectomies + 36 castrations). This direct administrative count demonstrates a substantial **internal reproduction-disqualifying practice** against persons classified white. The table counts **operations**, not necessarily 4,218 unique patients; no person-level deduplication is possible from this aggregate table. It cannot establish how many births were prevented: some patients had existing children, some procedures may have had no incremental demographic effect, coerced consent was systematically obscured, and age-specific person-years at risk are absent.

**Source:** [1964–1966 North Carolina primary report](https://archive.org/stream/biennialreporteug16nort/biennialreporteug16nort_djvu.txt), printed p. 30, historical Table 16. Keep the state's racial wording within quotation/context, not as our racial ontology.

### Number 2: the targets *changed*: white share 81.79% to 34.83% of operations (−46.96 percentage points)

In the **1946–1948** biennium, the reconstructed reports record **238 white / 53 Black / 0 Indigenous**, total 291: 238/291 = **81.79% white**. In **1964–1966** they record **124 white / 228 Black / 4 Indigenous**, total 356: 124/356 = **34.83% white**. The contrast is **−46.96 percentage points**, while the recorded Black share moves from 53/291=18.21% to 228/356=64.04%. This is a comparison of the **composition of people sterilized**, *not* race-specific population risk (no age-sex/class/marital denominators here) or a time trend in total fertility.

**Sources:** Alfred L. Brophy and Elizabeth Troutman (2016), *North Carolina Law Review* 94, Appendix Table 5 (historical 1946–1966 biennia), [publisher record](https://scholarship.law.unc.edu/nclr/vol94/iss6/2/), with the 1964–1966 endpoint independently found in the [Board's primary report](https://archive.org/stream/biennialreporteug16nort/biennialreporteug16nort_djvu.txt), printed p. 9.

The result complicates rather than confirms a simple "ever-narrower white purity" story. An initially substantial campaign directed against people recorded as white was later redirected dramatically toward Black residents. That is evidence of historically changing reproductive priorities and racialized administrative control. The precise causes, whether welfare-agency changes, class sorting, local politics or evolving racial objectives, require causal research.

In the same late biennium **261/356 = 73.31%** of people sterilized had already borne children, and **298/356 = 83.71%** were noninstitutional cases petitioned from county public-welfare departments. Neither fraction has a race cross-tabulation in the cited narrative; attributing either exclusively to white victims would be a mistake. Their institutional implication is narrower and firmer: sterilization was applied to existing families and welfare-system clients, not only to hypothetical future parents. [Primary report](https://archive.org/stream/biennialreporteug16nort/biennialreporteug16nort_djvu.txt), printed pp. 8–9.

### Number 3 (counterevidence): the population classified white in North Carolina still rose 13.95% from 1950 to 1960

The U.S. Census Bureau's historical Table 48 gives North Carolina white-category headcounts **2,983,121 in 1950** and **3,399,285 in 1960**, an increase of **416,164** persons, or **13.95%** of the 1950 count. Its recorded share in total state population rose from 73.4% to 74.6%. This does **not** show that sterilization did no individual harm or had no marginal effect on births. It *does* refute an easy inference from sterilizing thousands of nominal whites to aggregate white-category demographic collapse in this case. The change could include births, deaths, migration and classification effects; the aggregate census counts do not separate them. The comparison is not a matched-cohort causal estimate.

**Source:** Campbell Gibson and Kay Jung (2002), [Census Table 48](https://www2.census.gov/library/working-papers/2002/demo/pop-twps0056/table48.pdf), source rows 1950 and 1960.

### Number 4: Utah archives independently identify 665 known white-classified victims of 830 cases, with missing race recorded

Tabery, Novak, Sarafraz, and Mansfield (2023), *Victims of eugenic sterilisation in Utah*, reconstruct **830 cases** under Utah's law (1925–1974), of which **665 explicitly recorded as non-Hispanic white**, **15** other classified backgrounds, and **150** have no usable race/ethnicity information. Thus 665/830 = **80.12% of all observed cases** and 665/680 = **97.79% of cases with known race**, *but 18.07% of race entries are missing*. We cannot infer that missingness is random; the possible white fraction of the 830 recorded cases is 80.12% to 98.19%, conditional on this recorded cohort being complete and all 150 missing statuses being alternatively nonwhite or white.

In **1961 Utah's legislature changed the rationale** for eugenic sterilization from a supposed scientifically identifiable genetic impairment to a more permissive classification of unfitness to parent, permitting the practice to continue after its scientific rationale weakened. This is direct evidence of **adaptive selection authority**, not direct evidence of endlessly tightening white racial purity. It offers a discriminating mechanism to study: when an ideology fails on scientific grounds, governance may shift criteria rather than stop reproductive control.

**Source:** [Tabery et al. (2023), Table 1 and discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10025421/). Its 830 confirmed Utah cases are not the same population or denominator as North Carolina's 6,851 recorded operations; do not pool them without a comparative ascertainment model.

### The user's two-"super specimens" limit has a quantitative genealogical contradiction

For **two unrelated, non-inbred diploid founders** A and B, each has only two copies per autosomal locus: at most **four founder alleles** at any such locus. If their children (full siblings) alone reproduce, the first sibling-mating descendants have an **expected inbreeding coefficient F = 1/4 = 25%** by the standard parent-coancestry pedigree formula: the two common unrelated founders each contribute (1/2)^(1+1+1) = 1/8 to the offspring's F. This is an expected identical-by-descent probability in that offspring, not a claim that 25% of children die or are infertile. New mutation, additional founders, immigration and technological intervention change assumptions. First-generation children of the original unrelated founders are **not** inbred by virtue of having the same two parents; the inbreeding emerges once they produce descendants together.

Therefore maximizing alleged *individual purity* is **not** a demographic or conservation-genetic recipe for a viable reproducing population. This mathematical result is independent of the nonexistent biological definition of a white human race: the hypothesis concerns **selection ideologies and social institutions**, not real racial breeding pools. Source for pedigree inbreeding definition: [Pemberton (2008), §2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2386891/). This calculation is a conditional theoretical bound, not historical evidence that white populations were reduced to two founders.

### Decision against the original hypothesis: what these numbers settle

1. **Supported:** eugenic governance claimed authority to restrict reproduction *within nominal whiteness* (NC 4,218 operations assigned to white-classified people; Utah at least 665 distinct known-white cases), demonstrating the internal frontier.
2. **Supported:** reproductive classification and intervention changed direction over time (North Carolina's near-47-point compositional shift; Utah's 1961 rationale revision), showing **mutable regimes**, not a static binary.
3. **Supported counterexample:** in the NC period the white-classified state's census population grew markedly even as the eugenic program sterilized white-classified persons. The evidence refutes claiming that white population collapse was demonstrated.
4. **Rejected limit in its literal human-population form:** two reproductively viable adult founders are not an adequate indefinitely sustainable sexually reproducing genetic population, under ordinary pedigree assumptions.
5. **Unassayed:** whether a policy *ratchet* endogenously restricted eligibility until it causally reduced aggregate reproduction, the number of births foregone per sterilization, changes in reproduction among nonsterilized white households, and whether any race-preserving regime actually sacrificed its population's continuity on the required scale.

**More research needed to estimate foregone births rather than sterilization operations:** longitudinal sterilization files linked to age/parity/consent and births before and after, lawful survivor protections, matched unaffected comparison groups, and age-sex-year cohort rates. Never multiply sterilization operations by an assumed fertility rate and report that speculative product as a measured result. The period composition series also needs the *eligible* population denominator to estimate race-specific targeting rather than shares among selected victims.
