# Client commission: white increase and settler social reproduction

**Allocation.** A client engaged 561 Group to study a two-part historical-sociological proposition. The opening concern is whether a regime calling itself white may spend the actual lives and reproductive opportunities of white-classified people to seek a deferred, allegedly exceptional reproductive future which the existing people never embody. The following concern asks how the settler society has really reproduced: its own births and survivorship, immigration of adults and prospective parents, and children born under hereditary slavery whose potential descendants are appropriated as property. A proposed estimate that 25% of all U.S. births in an early republic interval occurred to enslaved mothers was a *starting query to investigate*, not the commissioned endpoint. The 561 Group must preserve the original researcher's question and intellectual credit, not appropriate authorship from a client.

**Author attribution and publication restriction.** The researcher is discussed as the client in working communications. Their legal/byline identity, exact preferred formulation, right to quote their private statement, institutional affiliation, and scope of public disclosure have **not** been established by a research authorization. These manuscripts do not expose their private wording or invent consent. They are scholarly working drafts in a private Science source repository. No submission, public posting or contact with journals has occurred.

## Deliverable A — Historical-conceptual journal article

[The Future White: Selective Reproductive Ideals and the Continuity of Settler Rule](2026-10-17-manuscript-selective-futurity-and-settler-continuity.md).

**Proposed journal:** *Settler Colonial Studies*. **Contribution sought:** distinguish (i) numerical reproduction of a historically classified settler population, (ii) production of a perpetually deferred ideal set of legitimate progenitors, and (iii) reproduction of territorial, property and political authority. Test which objective is preserved **in specific decisions under conflict**, rather than anthropomorphizing whiteness as a biological species or claiming every racial ideology sought deliberate demographic self-extinction.

**Documentary anchor:** Franklin's 1751 wish for a narrowly classified white increase coupled to imperial settlement; Roosevelt's 1913 simultaneous natalism/negative selection; Nearing's 1911 quantity/claimed-quality rebuttal; Virginia's 1924 Pocahontas exception and the proposed 1926 broadening; Nicholls's 2026 documented coexistence of exclusionary and amalgamationist settler sociology. The Virginia legislative incident is a *negative case for unconditional purity escalation*: the institution could broaden criteria to preserve people whose elite status mattered. Do not call the House amendment an enacted statute without the bill's full legislative record.

**Novelty test:** This study must not merely restate Nicholls's 2026 result (both kinds of eugenic sociology serve the settler state) or Morgan's 2021 result (reproductive kinship expropriation constitutive of Atlantic capitalism). Its proposed incremental contribution is an explicit causal-discrimination framework for decisions when demographic growth, ideological purity and elite status conflict, including evidence of whose reproductive opportunities were sacrificed. The client's literal “two ideal reproducers before demographic disappearance” remains a conjectural limiting case; not a proved outcome. A strong reviewer might argue the paper currently offers **no original archival discovery** beyond reanalysis; the actual 1926 House and Senate records and at least one comparable jurisdiction are necessary for a genuinely strong historical novelty claim.

**Remaining acceptance gates:** recover original 1926 Virginia bills and journals, record introduced/amended/enacted distinctions; critically review the *whole* Nicholls and Younis 2026 texts, plus Morgan's treatment of demography and contemporaneous primary actors; reconstruct a source-specific policy decision chronology with exact quotations/page locations; establish plausible alternative decisions and effects; obtain client intellectual credit/rights; and undergo independent historical peer critique. Until then, a mature **research draft**, not submission-ready peer-reviewed scholarship.

## Deliverable B — Historical-demography data/research note

[Birth into Captivity and the Reproductive Generation: Accounting for U.S. Births, 1790–1859](2026-10-17-manuscript-captive-births-and-settler-reproduction.md).

**Proposed journal:** *The History of the Family* (data note, short research article, or replication/methodological note depending editor policy). **Contribution sought:** reproducibly calculate the fraction of births among historically reconstructed U.S. residents that occurred into hereditary slavery, *without* calling enslaved births “industrial breeding events” by default or confusing the category of the counted state with everyone living on Indigenous lands.

**Inputs:** Hacker 2020 Table 1 column 7 by decade; Haines–Sutch Table Aa9–Aa14 annual U.S. births in thousands from 1800 onward; Haines Table Aa15–Aa21 mid-decade population and national growth components. The annual 1790s U.S. birth column is missing; report a 55/1000 assumption on 4.52m mean population and 45–65/1000 sensitivity—not a historical certainty.

**Headline descriptive outcomes:** 5,167,522 / 30,038,000 = 17.20% in 1800–1849; 5,571,170 / 32,524,000 = 17.13% in 1790–1849; 7,437,068 / 44,218,000 = 16.82% in 1790–1859. Sensitivity of the latter two estimates to the **1790s denominator alone** yields 16.89–17.37% and 16.65–16.99%. These are *not* complete confidence intervals and cannot identify privately directed conceptions or the commercial value of childbirth.

**Critical alternatives:** Haines finds national growth predominantly natural over 1790–1860, so a claim that the entire 19th-century U.S. depended primarily on net migration is false. Local settler reproduction and the status of children of migrants require new linked records. Historical U.S. census coverage excludes many Indigenous inhabitants and changes by annexation: it does not represent all births on the territory. Morgan's “Refusing Demography” is a vital methodological critical response to reducing captive persons to numbers.

**Remaining acceptance gates:** independently reproduce both publishers' series under explicit licenses; model Hacker numerator assumptions and additional error; reconcile shifting historical boundaries and 1790s assumptions; perform period-specific consistency tests, including excluding late-seventies slave import counts from the birth numerator; secure external demography review; and publish an openly addressable, credited, fixed-version data/code deposit if the client authorizes publication. The project is a *source-reconstruction and interpretive audit*, not independent discovery of individual-level birth records.

## Publishing disposition and next scientific decision

A serious receiving decision is whether the strongest original contribution should be (1) a policy-decision study of when elite status overrode declared purity, (2) a comparative reproductive-social-structure analysis involving particular captive and migrant populations, or (3) an independent historical demographic re-estimation with higher-grade sources. The present corpus is sufficient to propose all three, not to claim they are equally proven. The two manuscripts were selected as the useful current minimum: one historical-conceptual argument and one quantitative measurement note, with separate falsifiers and scientific evaluation.

**Required internal receiving standard:** another researcher must be able to state the client's original hypothesis in its first clause, identify exactly which citations are primary and which merely summarize others, recompute each published figure from the declared sources, recover the negative Virginia case, name one causal explanation the papers have not excluded, and observe the actual Engineering reader consuming the exact Science data version. A passing local file read is not public deployment, and a locally coherent manuscript is not an accepted journal article.

**Publishing gate:** do not submit, publish under a human's name, quote the client, or announce an accepted result until the necessary authority and external scholarly receiving actually exist.
