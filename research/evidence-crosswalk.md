# White increase and settler social reproduction: bibliography and evidence crosswalk

This is the existing Sociology bibliography's *commission index*, not a rival bibliography. Every identifier below resolves to a distinct source-owned annotated record in this directory. A source's date, original genre, accessible text and reported evidence matter more than the number of references attached to a sentence. The received client research question concerns whether a racialized order can reproduce its authority by consuming the reproductive continuity of its purported beneficiaries while expropriating that of others; no source is counted as proving that conjecture merely because it discusses eugenics.

## Reading and evidence grades

**Primary-quoted:** contemporaneous enacted law, letter, article or administrative record read from a reproducible transcription. **Historical-demographic reconstruction:** published estimates with modeled assumptions, not direct birth registrations. **Publisher abstract only:** a scholarly publication located and abstract inspected; numerical detail or causal inference may not be asserted until full reading. **Historical synthesis:** reviewed secondary reconstruction, ideally tested against its quoted primaries. **Preprint:** scholarly draft that has not been assigned peer-reviewed status. **Research conjecture:** the client's proposal, explicitly not a finding. None of these grades is a moral ranking of speakers or an assertion of source neutrality.

**Derived claims must specify units.** Births (flows), enslaved persons or white census category populations (stocks), migration (net flow), people sterilized (possibly person counts), reported operations, and courts' claims upon possible future increase are **not** interchangeable.

## Bibliographic gaps and negative findings

The corpus now distinguishes **source found**, **original document read**, **abstract only**, **data independently extracted**, **independently replicated**, and **applied to a specific causal claim**. Published titles can be cited without implying the latter stages. The strongest remaining deficits are: original 1926 Virginia bill journals and contemporaneous news interventions; full Djeliid 2024 article's *remaining* cases, independently extracted; first case now traced to a USF-catalogued 1936 FWP interview; independent legal opinions underlying Alphonso 2023; longitudinal white fertility microdata rather than proxy conflation; credible comparative cohort denominators for specific settler populations; and birth-related economic/property valuation data. These are missing *research results*, not a reason to invent an absent record.

The 2026 client-origin theoretical conjecture is attributed **privately** in the [commission dossier](../notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-client-publication-commission.md); no claim of permission to publish the client's words or assign a human author by name is made here.

## 2026-10-17 case admission: Everett primary interview

WI-08 now has a bounded, first-person primary record rather than merely an author's abstract. The original [Everett–Randolph 1936 interview](everett-randolph1936.md) is USF special-collection item SFS0021855_00001 and the Library of Congress *Florida Narratives* reprint; the editor-mediated transcription reports enforced pairing at Jim McClain's plantation, while Louisa's own quoted account describes coercion, subsequent children, and later mutual life. [Reader (2023)](reader2023.md), p. 484, rejects treating coercion and later emotional attachment as mutually exclusive. [Djeliid (2024)](djeliid2024.md) introduces the same recollection with the year 1937, whereas the primary interview and USF catalogue give **8 October 1936**. This is a bounded dating conflict to settle against original image custody, not a reason to invalidate the testimony or quietly repeat the later date.

The machine-consumable **Science-owned source observation** is [Everett case extract](../../../../../data/records/2026-10-17-everett-directed-reproduction-case-extract.json), designed for the existing CAIRN JSON record-reading operation. File presence does not assert installation or scientific adjudication. No extrapolated plantation prevalence is reported.

## Prioritized causal countercontrols from the first-sentence premise

The prior question is not exhausted by fertility bans. Three new source-located paths into the material contradiction now have distinct grades: [Fraser 2016](fraser2016.md) (full theoretical essay read, explaining accumulation's capacity to exhaust social reproduction); [Cooper 2017](cooper2017.md) (publisher synopsis read; why family-preservation politics can coexist with privatized reproductive obligations); and [Kearney et al. 2022](kearney-levine-pardue2022.md) (empirical critique of simple economic/policy explanations for post-2007 fertility decline). This prevents “white extinction” from becoming a preselected conclusion or a single-cause story. The owning method and falsifiers are in [white fertility estimands](../notes/2026/10/17/2026-10-17-white-fertility-estimand-conflicts.md). The empirical exercise that would settle these paths is not yet executed.
